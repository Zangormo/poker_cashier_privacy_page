Privacy Policy for HomePoker Cashier

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the HomePoker Cashier Android application (the "App"), and who may have access to that data.

1. Data stored only on your device

HomePoker Cashier is a poker tracker for home games. All gameplay information — created games, player profiles, hand/session history, and statistics — is stored exclusively on your device, in a local database (Room / SQLite).

  • The App has no backend server of its own and does not transmit this data anywhere over the internet.
  • There is no sign-up, account, or authentication in the App — the developer does not collect or store this data on any server.
  • If Android's standard Auto Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up the App's data to your associated Google account. This is a feature of Android itself, controlled by your device and Google account settings, not something the developer collects or accesses.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob (third-party SDK)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. This is the only third-party SDK in the App that collects data. The developer does not collect this data directly — it is collected and processed by Google as an independent data controller, under Google's own privacy policy.

For ad delivery and targeting, AdMob may process:

  • advertising identifier (Advertising ID / AAID);
  • device information (model, OS version, language, network parameters);
  • IP address (used, among other things, for approximate location and fraud prevention);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

For details on what data Google collects and how it is used, see Google's official privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More information about how Google's advertising products work is also available at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — a consent mechanism shown before ads are served, compliant with GDPR and the IAB TCF standard.

  • On first launch (or when applicable regulation changes), you are shown a consent dialog where you can allow or restrict personalized advertising.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the App's privacy settings section ("Privacy options").
  • Even if you opt out of personalization, ads may still be shown, but they will be non-personalized (based on context rather than a user profile).

4. Not directed at children

The App is not directed at, and is not targeted to, children under the age of 13 (or 16, depending on jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with data, please contact us using the details below so we can take appropriate action regarding any information within our control.

5. Your control over your data

Since all gameplay data is stored locally, you are in full control of it: you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in Android system settings, or by uninstalling the App — either action permanently removes all locally stored data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if the SDKs used by the App change, or as required by law. The current version is always available at this URL, with the "last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
