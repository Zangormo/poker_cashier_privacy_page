Privacy Policy

1. Data stored only on your device

HomePoker Cashier is a HomePoker Cashier for home games. All gameplay information — created games, player names, buy-ins, chip counts, session history and settlement calculations — is stored exclusively on your device, in a local database (Room / SQLite).

  • The App has no backend server of its own. There is no code in the App that sends this data to us or to any third party over the internet.
  • There is no sign-up, account, or authentication in the App — we do not collect or store this data on any server, because no such server exists.

2. Sharing a game between devices

The App lets you transfer a game you created to another device running the App, for players who want to continue tracking the same session from a different phone. This works in two ways, both of which you must actively start:

  • QR code. The App encodes the game (game settings, and for each player: their name, seat, buy-ins, chip returns and timestamps) into a QR code shown on your screen. The other device scans it with its camera. This exchange happens directly between the two devices — nothing is uploaded to a server, and no internet connection is required. Your device does not save or access camera images beyond reading the code itself.
  • File transfer. Alternatively, the App can create a game file on your device and hand it to an app of your choice via Android's standard "Share" menu (e.g., messaging app, email, Bluetooth, cloud storage) — you choose the destination each time.
  • Sharing a settlement. You can also share the final settlement (player names and amounts owed) as plain text via Android's "Share" menu, or copy it to your clipboard.

In every case above, the recipient is chosen by you, not by the App, and the data travels through the app or service you selected — it does not pass through any server we operate.

3. Cloud backup (Android Auto Backup)

If Android's standard Auto Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up the App's local data — including your games database and purchase status — to your associated Google account (Google Drive), and restore it when you set up the App on a new device. This is a feature of Android itself, governed by your device and Google account settings, not something we collect or have access to. You can disable this for the App at any time in your device's Android system settings.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

The App displays ads using Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). This is the only third-party SDK in the App that collects data. We do not collect this data directly — it is collected and processed by Google as an independent data controller, under Google's own privacy policy.

For ad delivery and targeting, AdMob and its supporting Google services may process:

  • advertising identifier (Advertising ID / AAID) and App Set ID;
  • device information (model, OS version, language, network parameters);
  • IP address (used, among other things, for approximate location and fraud prevention);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

For details on what data Google collects and how it is used, see Google's official privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More information about how Google's advertising products work is also available at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — a consent mechanism shown before ads are served, compliant with GDPR and the IAB TCF standard.

  • On first launch (or when applicable regulation changes), you are shown a consent dialog where you can allow or restrict personalized advertising.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the App's Privacy options setting.
  • Even if you opt out of personalization, ads may still be shown, but they will be non-personalized (based on context rather than a user profile).

6. In-app purchase: Remove Ads

The App offers a single one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — these are handled directly by Google Play. A flag recording whether you purchased "Remove Ads" is stored locally on your device (and may be included in Android Auto Backup as described in Section 3); the authoritative record of your purchase is kept by Google Play, not by us.

7. Notifications

The App can show local reminders (e.g., "Bomb pot!" timers) during a game. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device using Android's built-in alarm system — no server or push notification service is involved, and no data leaves your device to make this work.

8. Analytics, crash reporting, other SDKs

The App does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising-network SDK other than Google AdMob, UMP, and Google Play Billing described above (no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or similar). We do not track your usage of the App beyond what these Google SDKs may independently collect as described in Sections 4–6.

9. Age restriction

The App is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. Although the App does not facilitate real-money gambling, betting, or payments of any kind (see Terms of Use, Section 3), it is themed around poker and home-game cash tracking, and by using the App you confirm that you meet this minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the App and provided data through it, please contact us at the address below.

10. Your control over your data

Since all gameplay data is stored locally, you are in full control of it: you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in Android system settings, or by uninstalling the App — either action permanently removes all locally stored data from your device (this does not affect a Google Auto Backup copy already made; see Section 3 for how to manage that).
